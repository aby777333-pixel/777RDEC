Raptor has been in the hands of brokers and desks for a while without having a
public front door. This site is that front door.

The part worth your time is **Experience Raptor** — the sandbox. It is not a
video and not a screenshot gallery. It is the interface, running on a
deterministic price simulation. You can place an order, watch profit and loss
move, lower a risk limit until the ticket refuses your next order, arm the
intelligence layer inside a mandate you write, and disarm it.

Everything in it is simulated and labelled as such. No live account, no live
market data, no real money.

## Also new

- **EMIL Lab** — the intelligence layer's real control surface, where arming
  requires reading the authorisation text and typing a confirmation.
- **A security page that lists controls rather than badges.** We publish no
  certification logos. Where we hold something, we will name it and evidence it
  on request.
- **A technology-provider notice.** We are not a broker, and the site says so
  on every page rather than in a footnote.

If something on the site is unclear or wrong, tell us. Documentation and
marketing gaps are bugs.
